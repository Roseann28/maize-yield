AI assistance was used exclusively during the soil data preparation stage of this project.

Specifically, AI was used to assist with:

- Extracting soil-related data from the soil dataset.
- Mapping soil coordinates to the geographical coordinates of the One Acre Fund survey locations to match soil information with the corresponding farm locations.
